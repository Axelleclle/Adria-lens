Licence conditions — please read before use

One defined use

The licence applies to the buyer and the named website, publication/edition or campaign described in the agreement. A new outlet, edition, campaign or medium may require an additional licence.

Non-exclusive rights

The same photograph may be licensed to other customers. No offer on this page grants exclusivity, ownership of the image or transfer of copyright.

Not included

Resale, transfer to another organisation, paid advertising outside the campaign licence, and uses outside the stated scope are excluded. Ask for written permission before using the photo in another way.

The thinking behind the prices

One photo library. Four ways to license it.

A single €40 price treats a social post, a news article, a printed publication and a commercial campaign as if they created the same value. The case data suggest they do not. The proposed strategy uses value-based pricing (price linked to the value of the use) and price fences (clear licence limits that distinguish the offers). The price explanations above show how each recommendation relates to the case data; the figures are proposals to test, not proven market prices.

How the scenario is calculated

For each segment, the selected price is multiplied by the demand volume reported at that price in the case. Revenue is then reduced by variable costs: 40% photographer payments + 2% platform/payment fees + €0.50 processing and delivery per licence.

What the comparison means

The uniform-price scenario applies €40 to the demand points stated for that price. The segmented scenario applies the four proposed prices and their corresponding case demand points. Both results are modelled estimates, not guaranteed sales or a validated forecast.

Assumptions and limitations: annual fixed costs are €100,000; customer segments are treated as non-overlapping; the mix of licences is assumed to remain constant for break-even analysis. The case’s survey figures are fictional case inputs, not a real market study. The model does not fully include customer acquisition, administration beyond the stated processing cost, or licence-enforcement costs. The €8 digital editorial offer should therefore be piloted and reviewed against actual customer response and value.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Does the price include the licence?

Yes. The photo and its licence are sold together. The displayed price is the total price per photo licence, excluding VAT.

Can I use the same photo for different purposes?

The licence covers only the stated buyer, medium and scope. For example, a print licence does not cover web or e-paper use, and an editorial licence does not cover paid advertising. If you want to use a photo in another medium, publication or unrelated campaign, obtain an additional or extended licence before that use.

Why are the prices different?

The intended use and commercial value differ by segment. The case indicates strong price sensitivity among occasional buyers, high potential volume for digital portals at lower prices, willingness to pay around €36–€40 among print publishers, and higher stated willingness to pay for advertising use. These case inputs support differentiated offers, but real demand should be tested.

Are the licences exclusive?

No. All offers shown on this page are non-exclusive. The buyer receives only the stated usage rights; copyright is not transferred.

What if I need a different type of use?

Contact Adria Lens before using the photo outside the scope described in your licence so that the appropriate rights can be discussed.
